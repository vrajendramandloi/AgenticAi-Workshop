Curated Day-by-Day Itinerary & Visiting Places

Day 1: Arrival in Zurich & Altstadt Sunset Yacht

Arrive at Zurich Airport from Mumbai. Private VIP transfer to The Dolder Grand. Evening private walking tour of Zurich Old Town (Altstadt) followed by an exclusive sunset yacht cruise on Lake Zurich with champagne.

Day 2: Alpine Mastery – Mount Titlis & Engelberg

Scenic journey to Engelberg. Experience the world's first rotating cable car (Rotair) up to Mount Titlis for high-altitude skiing, snow parks, and panoramic alpine views. Return to Zurich for a 2-Michelin-starred dinner at The Restaurant at The Dolder Grand.

Day 3: Scenic Transfer to Interlaken & Lauterbrunnen Valley

Private transfer to Interlaken, checking into the majestic Victoria-Jungfrau Grand Hotel & Spa. Afternoon private chauffeured tour through the breathtaking Lauterbrunnen Valley ("Valley of 72 Waterfalls").

Day 4: Top of Europe – Jungfraujoch & Grindelwald First

VIP cogwheel train excursion to Jungfraujoch (Top of Europe) and the Sphinx Observatory. Afternoon thrill at Grindelwald First with cliff walks and alpine ropeway adventures. Evening thermal spa relaxation.

Day 5: Harder Kulm Sunset & Departure

Morning funicular ride to Harder Kulm for panoramic views over Interlaken. Leisurely shopping for Swiss chocolates and souvenirs before private transfer to Zurich Airport for your return flight to Mumbai.
